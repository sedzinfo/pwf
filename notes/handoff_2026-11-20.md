## SEM labels, equality constraints and definitions (done later on 20/11)
_sem.py now fits labelled models as lavaan 0.7.3 (it raised NotImplementedError before):
- Parsing reuses _ordinal's terms (labels, start(), NA*) and linear `==` parser; `:=` definitions are R
  arithmetic of labels (+ - * / ^, sqrt/exp/log/abs) evaluated safely (_evaluate). Inequalities and non-linear
  constraints still raise NotImplementedError.
- Parameters sharing a label share one value; `==` constraints become a reparameterisation (values = offset +
  J theta, the optimizer moving theta); standard errors J (J' I J)^-1 J' (lavaan's augmented inverse), with
  the observed information under FIML. A label shared with a fixed parameter fixes them all, as lavaan.
- Outputs as lavaan: npar = free parameters less constraints (df gains one each); parameter_estimates() has
  a label column, the := rows (delta-method SEs by complex step; std.lv/std.all from the standardized values)
  and, with remove_eq=False (rwf's report_cfa), the user's == rows (est lhs - rhs, se 0); lavaan's row numbers
  as index (its hidden == rows of shared labels skipped); R squared in the order of the variance rows, NA
  above 1 (lav_inspect_rsquare); modification indices under the constraints, numbered after the definition
  and constraint rows. report_cfa puts exo after label; plot_cfa_gg(what="eq") works on labelled models and
  plot_cfa then gives rwf's nine diagrams.
- Section "LAVAAN LABELS AND CONSTRAINTS": 7 models (shared loadings, a constraint with a constant, equal
  residual variances, a structural model with ==, := and shared labels, std.lv, a label shared with the
  marker, FIML) plus the report_cfa table and the eq diagrams. Estimates agree to 7e-5 and MIs to 2.4e-3
  (lavaan stops earlier: its chi-square is a hair above pwf's in every case, and the gap shrinks when
  lavaan's rel.tol is tightened).
- SEs that can't be computed (information not positive definite, as in an unidentified model) are missing
  with a warning, as lavaan's NA.
