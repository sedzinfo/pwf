## Done
- `_lbfgsb.py`, new: R's `optim(method = "L-BFGS-B")`, ported from lbfgsb.c (version 2.3), optim.c and
  LINPACK's dpofa/dtrsl.
  - Why it's needed: psych optimizes `fm = "wls"`, `"gls"`, `"minchi"` and `"old.min"` with the ML
    gradient, not their own. R's answer is therefore where its line searches stop, 0.02 to 0.29 away from
    the real optimum. scipy's L-BFGS-B (version 3.0) leaves R's path near the bounds.
  - Checked bit for bit against R's own `optim` on the same functions (through rpy2, reference BLAS): 240
    random problems, numerical gradients, maxit stops, and 48 psych fits.
  - Pinned by section "R'S OPTIM L-BFGS-B" (8 problems) and `tests/test_lbfgsb.py`.
- `_efa.fa`:
  - every psych `fm` except `"minrank"` (psych's objectives, gradients, starts, bounds and loadings; the pa
    and alpha iterations);
  - rotations: stats::varimax, psych's promax/Promax and equamax, and GPArotation's Varimax, quartimax,
    bentlerT, geominT, oblimin, quartimin, simplimax, geominQ and bentlerQ (GPForth and GPFoblq with their
    Barzilai-Borwein steps);
  - `oblique_scores`, `min_err`, `max_iter` and `warnings`, plus psych's naming of the factors (PA1, ULS1,
    oldmin1, ...; ols unnamed, shown as X1);
  - psych's messages, warnings and errors: an unknown fm becomes minres; an unknown rotation fails;
    alpha factoring of one factor fails as in psych;
  - every optimized method, ml and minres included, now goes through the R port;
  - the RMSEA bounds use `r_uniroot` with R's tolerance, as psych's `uniroot()` does.
- Section "EFA METHODS AND ROTATIONS" and `tests/test_efa_methods.py` (95 tests):
  - 60 models: 10 methods × 1–3 factors × complete and missing data;
  - 28 models: 14 rotations × 2–3 factors;
  - psych's errors, and `report_efa` on pa, ols and wls models.
  - Largest loading differences from psych: 1e-11 everywhere, 6.5e-8 for ols (which uses `optim`'s
    numerical gradient).
- `_fit_efa` passes every `fa()` option through.

## Findings (also in TODO.md)
- psych rotates from 20 starts (random ones from R's random numbers); pwf from the identity. The two agree
  unless the random starts reach another solution: quartimax, geomin and simplimax did on mtcars. The
  references therefore use `n.rotations = 1` (not needed for varimax, promax and equamax).
- References for wls/gls/minchi/old.min depend on R's BLAS: one model moves 3.4e-4 under OpenBLAS.
  Generate them on the reference BLAS.
- psych bug, copied: alpha factoring of one factor fails unless the first eigenvalue is between 1 and 4.
