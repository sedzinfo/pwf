## SEM with observed predictors (done later on 23/11)
`_sem.py` now fits regressions on and of observed variables as lavaan 0.7.3 (it raised NotImplementedError):
- Variable classes and order as `lav_pt_vnames`:
  - indicators, then outcomes, then predictors (`ov.x`), then variables named only in covariances;
  - a predictor the syntax gives a variance or covariance is random, with lavaan's warning.
- Automatic rows as `lav_pt_flat`:
  - `auto.cov.y`: the residuals of outcomes that predict nothing covary. This was missing before, also for
    latent outcomes: `E ~ age; N ~ age` now has `E ~~ N` as lavaan;
  - the predictors' block last; exogenous latent variables don't covary with observed predictors.
- `fixed.x` (`sem(..., fixed_x=True)`, the default):
  - the predictors' (co)variances and means are fixed at the sample values (divisor N), `exo` rows;
  - their moments leave the degrees of freedom; the baseline model keeps their block saturated (also with
    `fixed_x=False`);
  - `logl` is conditional on them, i.e. without their marginal likelihood;
  - the expected information zeroes their block of dSigma, which is what lavaan's modification indices use;
  - FIML drops cases without a predictor, with lavaan's warning.
- lavaan's LISREL representation (`lav_lisrel`): observed variables in regressions, and those sharing a
  covariance with one or with a latent variable, are dummy latent variables. `inspect()` returns lavaan's
  matrices with them, plus `alpha` next to `nu` (also new for FIML models). Derivatives and means follow the
  representation.
  - A covariance's derivative is the product of the two variables' "effect vectors". That matters for a
    modification-index candidate between a dummy and an indicator, which lavaan turns into a dummy too.
- `parameter_estimates`: lavaan's `std.nox` column with fixed predictors; `std.lv` scales by real latent
  variables only. R squared is lavaan's: 1 − std.all of the variances of indicators and outcomes.
- `predict()` follows `lav_predict_eta_normal`:
  - latent means from `lav_lisrel_alpha0` without a mean structure;
  - E(y) from `lav_lisrel_nu0`, which is shifted when a model has outcome dummies but no predictor
    dummies. That is lavaan's behaviour and pwf copies it;
  - Λ with each outcome dummy's row replaced by its row of B (`lav_lisrel_lambda`).
- GFI (LISREL) uses lavaan's conditional weights; AGFI and PGFI use every sample moment (p(p+1)/2).
- `report_cfa`'s `exo` column now comes from the fitted model (0 for free predictors).
- Section "LAVAAN OBSERVED PREDICTORS" and `tests/test_sem_observed_predictors.py` (55 tests):
  - 12 models: MIMIC, a direct effect, two outcomes, mediation, a pure path, an observed outcome of a latent,
    an observed mediator, `fixed.x = FALSE`, random predictors, FIML with missing predictors, `std.lv`,
    labels with `:=`;
  - each compared on parameters, all fit measures, R squared, modification indices, matrices and factor
    scores, plus rwf's `report_cfa` and diagrams of the direct-effect model.
  - Estimates agree to 2e-4. MIs agree to 1e-3 relative, because lavaan stops a little earlier.
  - Equal MIs (a covariance and the equivalent regression) sort by their last bits, so they are compared by
    parameter.
- Not done: `simulate_data` with observed regressions, `conditional.x`, higher-order factors. The FIML robust
  measures, CRMR and GFI family stay missing as before.

## SEM mean structures with complete data (done later on 23/11)
- `meanstructure = TRUE` now works with complete data: the ML discrepancy, its gradient and the score gain
  (ybar - mu)' Sigma^-1 (ybar - mu); the expected information gains dmu' Sigma^-1 dmu (SEs of intercepts, their
  MIs); logl and the baseline df include the means.
- Intercepts in the syntax, as lavaan: `y ~ 1`, `y ~ 0*1`, `y ~ a*1`, a free latent mean `F ~ 1`. They imply a
  mean structure. User rows keep their place; the automatic intercepts follow the variances (observed first,
  then latent means fixed at 0). A predictor given an intercept is random, as lavaan.
- Fit measures ported from lavaan's `lav_fit_*` functions, including their edge rules (df = 0 used to crash
  `rmsea.ci.lower`):
  - `gfi` from Browne's residual statistic (`lav_test_browne`, with lavaan's fixed.x Gamma
    `lav_samp_gamma_nt`), or from the chi square under FIML;
  - `gfi_lisrel` with lavaan's ML weights (`lav_mvn_info_expected`: the predictors' rows zeroed, means
    included); AGFI and PGFI use every moment;
  - the RMSEA and GFI intervals with R's `uniroot` brackets;
  - NFI, RFI, CN and the RMSEA p values at df = 0;
  - CRMR's mean residuals scaled by the observed SDs.
  - Under FIML, CRMR and `gfi` now match lavaan. `gfi_lisrel`, `agfi_lisrel` and `pgfi` stay missing: the
    per-pattern h1 information comes within 1.3e-5 but isn't lavaan's yet.
- Section "LAVAAN MEAN STRUCTURES" and `tests/test_sem_mean_structure.py` (38 tests):
  - 9 cases: CFA, a latent SEM, MIMIC, a path model, std.lv, a free latent mean, labelled and fixed
    intercepts (a non-saturated mean structure), a saturated model (df = 0), and FIML with a free latent mean;
  - plus rwf's `report_cfa`.
  - The checks shared with the observed-predictor tests moved to `tests/_lavaan_checks.py`.
